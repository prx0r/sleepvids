# generationguide.md — YouTube Channel Operations & AI Slop Compliance

## Channel Setup (The Goat Setup)

One Google account. Separate Brand Account per channel. One AdSense (required — duplicates get banned).

Brand accounts give per-channel analytics/managers, one login to rule them, and channel deletion doesn't nuke the Google account. This is the dominant advice for legit multi-channel operators.

Differentiation is your firewall, not email separation. Distinct branding, distinct formats, no cross-posted content. "Related channels" fires on sameness.

Quality moat per channel: human-written scripts, real editing, no TTS-over-static. Keep receipts that a human made each video (project files, drafts, research).

Phone verification: ~6 Google accounts per number; Brand Accounts don't each need a number.

**Don't worry about the email.** Worry about any single channel earning the "inauthentic" label — that's the contagious one.

## The AI Slop Crackdown (2026)

### What happened
- January 2026: Mass removals. 16 channels (35M subs, 4.7B views) swept. 11M subs wiped across big AI channels.
- April 2026: "Inauthentic content" policy wave. Faceless channels demonetized for TTS-over-slideshow and regurgitated scripts.
- September 2026: ~21% of recommended videos qualify as slop by one methodology. Enforcement at scale.
- CEO named slop a top-2026 priority.

### The three buckets of "inauthentic" (clarified July 16, 2026)
1. **Generic/repetitive/template-based** — cookie-cutter videos with little variation, regardless of tool. Tutorials reproducing prevalent content count too.
2. **Off-putting/distressing** — engineered emotional manipulation (fake rescues etc.).
3. **AI personas on sensitive topics** — AI people discussing health, finance, legal.

### What separates survivors (from appeal winners + reviewers)
- **Production fingerprint, not the plot.** Reviewers judge the channel pattern — 20 uploads from one recipe reads industrial even with different stories. Vary the engine, not just the topic.
- **Same voice as 10,000 channels = flag.** The shared ElevenLabs-preset sound is a detection signal. Custom/niche voice or your own voice wins.
- **Editing proof.** Raw AI output with no transforms dies; visible human editing (pacing decisions, original structure, real retention craft) lives.
- **Specificity.** Sourced, specific claims beat generic narration — slop detection is literally trained on generic.
- **Appeals work when you show the work.** Winners film themselves, show channel URL in 30s, walk through editing process, prove no templates. Keep project files, drafts, research — receipts before you need them.

### Collateral damage
Human faceless creators get swept (300M-view lore channel, no AI, hit twice). Appeals reversed most documented cases — but it takes weeks and earnings don't always backfill.

## Our Compliance Strategy (sleepvids)

### What we do that's green-flagged
- **Longform content** — 60-120 min videos, not 60-second shorts
- **Human-written scripts** — sourced, researched, specific claims
- **Distinct visual identity per channel** — not the same template everywhere
- **Produced cadence** — not 10 uploads/day
- **NoSlop pipeline** — quality gate that catches generic patterns
- **Research receipts** — RESEARCH.md, drafts, sections/ prove human authorship
- **Sourced scripts** — every claim traceable to a source (Etymonline, Knuth, papers)

### What to avoid
- Same narration voice across all channels (ElevenLabs preset = detection signal)
- Same visual template across channels (related + templated = double hit)
- Mass-produced narration-over-stock
- Raw AI output with no transforms
- Same upload schedule/format across channels

### Per-channel differentiation
| Channel | Voice | Visual | Format |
|---------|-------|--------|--------|
| Etymology | V03 Lecturer | Black screen | 60-120 min essay |
| Interesting Tribes | V01 Sage or V07 Navigator | Field footage/ambient | 45-60 min profile |
| (future) | (varies) | (varies) | (varies) |

### Receipts to keep
- `/sections/` — proves human wrote each section
- `/RESEARCH.md` — proves sources were consulted
- `/STORYBOARD.md` — proves planning happened
- `/meta.json` — proves NoSlop quality gate ran
- Git history — proves incremental human authorship

## The "Related Channels" Rule

If one channel gets YPP-suspended for inauthentic/reused content, YouTube's email literally says none of your channels (including new ones) can monetize until it's resolved. Separate Gmails don't dodge this; AdSense + device signals reconnect you.

Separation helps with:
- Hacking (one breach doesn't take all)
- Strikes containment at the margins

It does NOT help with:
- Identity (AdSense links everything)
- Related channel flags (device fingerprints reconnect you)

**The real killer isn't which email — it's making sure no single channel earns the "inauthentic" label.**

## Differentiation Rules

1. **Different voice per channel** — not just different settings, different voice entirely
2. **Different visual identity** — different colors, fonts, intro style
3. **No cross-posting** — each channel's content lives only on that channel
4. **Different upload cadence** — don't upload all channels on the same day
5. **Distinct title/thumbnail style** — each channel should look like a different creator made it
6. **Different aspect ratios if possible** — some channels 16:9, some 9:16, some mix
