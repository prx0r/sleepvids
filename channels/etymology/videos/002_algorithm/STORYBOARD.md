# STORYBOARD — "The Word 'Algorithm'"

## Scene 1: The Hook
**Time:** 0:00-2:00 | **Words:** ~210
**Tone:** Surprising, quotable
**Content:** Today, "the algorithm" decides what you see, what you buy, who you meet. But the word started as a man's name. A Persian mathematician in 9th-century Baghdad. His name was mangled, corrupted, and became the invisible force that runs your life.

## Scene 2: The Map
**Time:** 2:00-4:00 | **Words:** ~210
**Tone:** Inviting, cozy
**Content:** We'll follow this word from a man in Uzbekistan to a book in Baghdad, through Latin translators, through French scribes, through English corruption, into computing, into your phone.

## Scene 3: The Cozy Opening
**Time:** 4:00-6:00 | **Words:** ~210
**Tone:** Warm, settling in
**Content:** Introduce al-Khwarizmi. The House of Wisdom. The man behind the word.

## Scene 4: The Man and His Book
**Time:** 6:00-20:00 | **Words:** ~1,470
**Tone:** Historical, biographical
**Content:** Who al-Khwarizmi was. Khwarazm → Baghdad. The House of Wisdom. His two books. Algebra and algorithm from the same person. The irony that his methods were replaced but his name survived.

## Scene 5: The Journey of the Word
**Time:** 20:00-45:00 | **Words:** ~2,625
**Tone:** Follow-the-thread, surprising
**Content:** Arabic → Latin (Algoritmi) → Old French (algorisme) → Middle English (algorism) → 1690s corruption to "algorithm" (Greek arithmos influence). The spelling lie. The -th- that shouldn't be there. Two words, one man.

## Scene 6: The Modern Demon
**Time:** 45:00-70:00 | **Words:** ~2,625
**Tone:** Reflective, philosophical
**Content:** From "any computation method" to "the invisible force." Donald Glover thanking the algorithm. Uber drivers calling it the boss. The loop: person → method → system → demon. Zero → binary → computing → AI.

## Scene 7: The Return
**Time:** 70:00-80:00 | **Words:** ~1,050
**Tone:** Soft, circular
**Content:** The man from Khwarazm. His name in your mouth every day. The loop closes. Sleep well.
