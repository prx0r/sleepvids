# SCAFFOLD — "The Word 'Algorithm'"

## The one-sentence story
A Persian mathematician's name was mangled through Latin, corrupted by Greek, and became the word for the invisible force that decides what you see on your phone tonight.

## The arc

```
780 CE: Born in Khwarazm (Uzbekistan)
    ↓
820 CE: Writes algebra book in Baghdad's House of Wisdom
    ↓
12th century: Latin translation mangles his name → "Algoritmi"
    ↓
13th century: Old French "algorisme" → Middle English "algorism"
    ↓
1690s: Spelling corrupted to "algorithm" (Greek arithmos influence)
    ↓
1930s: Broadens from "Arabic numerals" to "any computation method"
    ↓
Mid-20th century: Becomes the word for computing procedures
    ↓
2018+: "The algorithm" — the invisible force that controls your feed
```

## The loops

### Loop 1: Person → Method → System → Demon
al-Khwarizmi was a person. His methods became "algorithm." The method became a system. The system became a demon. The word went from a name to a noun to a force.

### Loop 2: The spelling lie
The -th- in "algorithm" comes from Greek arithmos. But the word has nothing to do with Greek arithmetic. It's a Persian name, mangled through Latin, corrupted by Greek.

### Loop 3: Two words, one man
The same man gave us "algorithm" AND "algebra." Two of the most important words in mathematics. Both from the same person.

### Loop 4: The word outlived the work
Al-Khwarizmi's algorithms were replaced after 300 years. But his name survived. The word outlived the method.

### Loop 5: From Baghdad to your phone
A man in 9th-century Baghdad wrote a book about equations. That book was translated to Latin in the 12th century. The translator mangled his name. The mangled name became a word. The word became the invisible force that decides what you see tonight.

### Loop 6: Zero → binary → computing → AI
Al-Khwarizmi introduced zero to the West. Zero is the foundation of binary. Binary is the foundation of computing. The man who gave us zero gave us the word for the thing that now uses zero to think.
